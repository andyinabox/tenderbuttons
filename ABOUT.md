This project is an homage to Gertrude Stein's 1914 book [_Tender Buttons_](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL7091921M/Tender_buttons). She reflected on the her writing process during this era in a [1946 interview](https://archive.org/details/primerforgradual0000stei/page/18/mode/1up):

> I took individual words and thought about them until I got their weight and volume complete and put them next to another word, and at this same time I found out very soon that there is no such thing as putting them together without sense. It is impossible to put them together without sense. I made innumerable efforts to make words write without sense and I found it impossible. Any human being putting down words had to make sense out of them.

The sentences and phrases you'll find here are produced using a very simple [Markov chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_chain#Markov_text_generators) fed with the contents of Stein's book.

You can view the source code for this project [here](https://code.andydayton.com/andy/tenderbuttons).

—[Andy](https://andydayton.com)
